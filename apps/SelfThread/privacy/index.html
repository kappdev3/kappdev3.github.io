Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the SelfThread app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Kappdev (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a free app with optional paid subscriptions. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), from which your approximate location (city/country) is derived
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The actions you perform in the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • App installation identifiers and your device's advertising ID
  • Purchase and subscription information

The Application does not access your device's GPS or precise location. Approximate location derived from your IP address is used only for aggregated analytics.

When you add a link to a message, the Application contacts that website to load a preview, and that website can see your IP address.

The Application does not require an account and does not ask you to provide personally identifiable information. If you choose to contact the Service Provider by email, they receive your email address and the contents of your message, and use them only to respond to you.

Subscriptions and Purchases

The Application offers optional paid subscriptions. Payments are processed by Google Play. The Service Provider does not receive your payment card details.

To verify and manage your subscription, the Application uses RevenueCat, which acts on behalf of the Service Provider. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, an anonymous app user ID, and basic information about your device settings and how you interact with subscription offers. The Application does not require an account, so the Service Provider cannot link this information to you personally.

App Configuration

The Application uses Firebase Remote Config to download app settings. To select the right values, Firebase uses an app installation identifier.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data, such as your email address and the contents of emails you send to the Service Provider, for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. If you'd like them to delete User Provided Data that you have provided via the Application, please contact them at kappdev3@gmail.com and they will respond in a reasonable time.

Automatically Collected Information held by the third-party services listed above is retained according to their policies: Google Analytics for Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months, Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days, Firebase Remote Config keeps app installation identifiers until they are deleted, and RevenueCat keeps purchase information according to its privacy policy. The Service Provider only uses anonymous identifiers and cannot identify you from them, so they cannot locate this information for a specific person or delete it on request. It is deleted by these services as described above. Uninstalling the Application resets its app installation identifiers.
